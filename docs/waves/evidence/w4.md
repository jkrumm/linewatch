| Condition | Result |
|-|-|
| absent vs clean are both neutral and separated by alpha only | absent is `VX.accent` (blue), clean is `VX.neutral` (grey): different hue in both schemes (`raw/w4-dark-1280-24h.png`, `w4-light-1280-24h.png`) |

| the absent legend toggle zeroes the share | `legend.toggle: false` on both strips and the bars: legend entries are not buttons in the a11y tree |

**Throughput is weaker:** `MirroredBars` paints its absent share in a fixed `VX.neutral` and takes no
`absentState`. It differs from Download (accent) but is a grey like Upload (`VX.line2`); what separates
them is that absent is a full-height bar on both panes. Recorded as basalt friction, not worked around.
