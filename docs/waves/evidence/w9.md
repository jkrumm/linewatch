## Tooltip text vs the baseline (7d, dark, 1280)

Same rows as 1.41 for latency (internet/anchors), speed, bufferbloat, throughput, link-speed. Changes, all intended 2.x:
headers are one format now ("Oct 12, 07:00 AM", the baseline mixed German and en-US); the strip's `Cloudflare` header
badge is an `Anchor | Cloudflare` row; `Carried` / `Link speed` header-row labels are gone (no `tooltip.label` slot);
every in-view chart shows its tooltip at once (as 1.41). Speed heatmap, three states by fill and text:
measured `This hour | 1.1% below the fastest hour | Download | 551.6 Mbps | Successful runs | 1`; failed
`This hour | Failed run | Failed runs | 1 — You may only use this Speedtest software and information generated`
(a banner rule of 80 `=` used to be the reason and squeezed the label column to one character a line — fixed by
skipping lines with no letter); absent `This hour | No run`. Availability heatmap: `Loss | 0.00% | Worst cycle | … |
Measured | 70 of 120 expected cycles`. Keyboard: focus alone shows the tooltip (1.41 did not); ArrowRight steps one bucket
and drives the strip + speed tooltips. Sparklines: still no tooltip (as 1.41).
